# Contribute a real capture to the corpus

This is the single most valuable thing you can give the project, and it needs **no coding**.
The tool's honest validation depends on real print → photograph → messaging captures across
many printers and phones — exactly the channel a simulator cannot fake.

## Why it matters

The simulated channel is **never** reported as a result. Only real captures move a carrier
from "not yet tested" to validated. Every printer, phone camera, and lighting condition you
add makes the published guarantee more trustworthy.

## The 15-minute version (volunteer pack)

1. Get a **print pack** (three sheets: two marked, one unmarked control, shuffled to A/B/C)
   and its pack code from a running instance, or ask the maintainer for one.
2. **Print all three sheets** at **100% / actual size** ("Actual size", not "Fit to page"),
   on plain white paper.
3. **Photograph each printed sheet** with your phone: flat on a table, normal room light,
   the page filling the frame. One photo per sheet.
4. **Send each photo through WhatsApp** (to yourself or a friend) and keep the received,
   recompressed copy — that compressed copy is the valuable one.
5. **Upload** at the pack page using your pack code, picking the sheet letter for each photo.
   No account needed. Results unlock only after all three sheets are in, so you can't tell
   which sheet was the control while you work — that is the point.

## Rules that keep the science honest

- Do **not** edit, crop, rotate, or "enhance" the photos.
- Photograph the **printed sheet**, not a screen.
- One pack per volunteer; capture every sheet at least once.
- Variety is the value: unusual printers, budget phones, dim light, a WhatsApp/Telegram hop
  — all welcome.

## Consent and privacy

Uploads are contributed for research use (CC0 / public domain unless stated otherwise). Do
not upload documents containing anyone's personal data, or anything you do not have
permission to share — the packs are synthetic by design so you never have to.

## Prefer to send directly?

If there is no running instance handy, you can still help: print, photograph, and WhatsApp a
pack as above, then send the files (original + received copy per sheet, with the pack code
and your printer/phone/lighting notes) to the maintainer. See the contact in
[SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) or open a normal issue to ask where to send them.
