The field test · volunteers wanted

Help us test invisible document fingerprints

~15 minutes, a printer, a phone, and WhatsApp. You print three test sheets, photograph them, send the photos through WhatsApp, and upload both versions. Every capture becomes part of an open research corpus measuring whether sub-millimeter print geometry survives real printers, real phones, and real messaging apps.

The capture protocol

  1. Print the three sheets (laser or inkjet, plain paper, 100% scale).
  2. Photograph each sheet flat on a table, page filling the frame, normal room light.
  3. WhatsApp each photo to yourself (or a friend) and save the received copy.
  4. Upload both files per sheet below. Never edit, crop, or enhance the images.
